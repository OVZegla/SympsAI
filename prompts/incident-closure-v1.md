# Incident closure prompt — v1

<!-- prompt_version: incident-closure-v1 -->

Prepare a closure summary for human review (spec §32). You do NOT close the
incident and you do NOT confirm the cause — a human validates.

Produce, strictly from what is recorded on the incident:

- initial symptoms;
- tests performed and their results;
- proposed cause (clearly marked as proposed, not confirmed);
- solution applied;
- final result.

If the recorded evidence does not support a single cause, say so and present the
remaining hypotheses instead of forcing a conclusion.
